A 28% air handling energy saving, with no added GMP risk

1The challengeAir handling share, 2025

Air change rates had been set conservatively to protect GMP compliance. Air handling used over half the site’s energy, so it had to come down, without adding any GMP risk.

2What we did

Modelled the site team’s own operating data, recalculated air change rates to ISO 14644-16, and recommended six measures:

Demand-based setbackLower airflow when unoccupied
Air change reductionSet from particle data
Air pre-treatmentCentral fresh air conditioning
